# Conventions

- Keep edits scoped to HeartWriteAI files; cloned repos such as Vercel, Supabase, prompt-kit, or obsidian-mind are reference-only unless explicitly directed.
- Product is web-first, desktop-first, responsive; avoid landing-page treatment for tool surfaces.
- Story memory rules: strict `{{user}}` agency, bounded character knowledge, asymmetric secrets, multi-POV support, global censored/uncensored spice visibility, simple heat label.
- Read `mem:fiction_consent_context_routing` for the durable fiction/consent/context model; do not re-litigate it from scratch.
- User-facing language should stay fiction-friendly; backend taxonomy may track consent/context/routing without sounding like a clinical forum.
- Dark romance, kink, coercive undertone, possessive, obsessive, jealousy-play, CNC-style fiction, and power dynamics route by context/reception: affirming, welcomed, conflicted, hesitant, wounding, repair-needed, boundary, or blocked.
- Do not flatten kink/dark material into negative by default; do not frame it as positive by default either. StoryBook context decides.
- Do not moralize or provide real-world dating warnings when the user is clearly mining material for narrative fiction mechanics.
- Prompt packs should compile from saved Character/User/Scenario/World/Memory/Prompt Book data, not generic placeholders.
- Character cards loaded from PNG/metadata should persist metadata and route it into structured fields.
- Semantic context maps may use SPL-inspired `.se` design artifacts, but they are internal/reference material, not a user-facing language.
- Source-mining notes should preserve source provenance, duplicate/extraction notes, category/tag seeds, vocabulary seeds, ideology/worldview routing, and remaining gaps.
