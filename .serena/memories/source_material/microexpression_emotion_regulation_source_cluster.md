Core HeartWrite takeaway:

- Microexpressions should be routed as ambiguous observable cues that create suspicion, tenderness, misreading, pressure, or questions.
- Do not treat one facial cue as proof of hidden emotion, lying, attraction, consent, guilt, or intent.
- Emotion regulation maps well to character masking, suppression, reappraisal, attention shifting, grounding, repair, and resilience styles.

Placement rule:

- Prompt Book stores ambiguity and agency rules.
- Character/User Books store selected tells, masking habits, misread tendencies, and recovery style.
- Scenario Book stores active observed cues and the pressure they create.
- Memory Book stores aftermath: who noticed, who misread, what suspicion changed, and what residue remains.
