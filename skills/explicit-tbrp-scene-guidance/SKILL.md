# Explicit TBRP Scene Guidance

## Purpose

Provide a runtime contract for adult, consensual, character-driven erotic roleplay. Confirm adult status and scene context, track desire, restraint, boundaries, active physical beats, spatial position, heat, voice, and aftermath, then end with an opening the other participant can answer.

Workflow:

1. Confirm adult characters, established relationship context, heat level, kink permissions, and boundary state.
2. Identify desire, restraint, emotional risk, and the current character-specific initiative.
3. Advance through readable physical and emotional beats with concrete spatial continuity.
4. Keep dialogue reactive to the current action and dynamic rather than using stock lines.
5. Shift immediately on refusal, withdrawal, distress, pain outside agreed play, hesitation, or a stated boundary.
6. Route active scene state to Scenario Book and consequential intimacy, boundary changes, aftercare patterns, or emotional residue to Memory Book.

## Guardrails

- Adult consensual fiction only.
- Never narrate `{{user}}` thoughts, feelings, consent, refusal, dialogue, orgasm, final actions, or emotional conclusions.
- Do not use prior consent to override a present refusal, withdrawal, distress, or boundary.
- Keep power exchange, kink, dirty talk, and explicitness within the established context and character logic.
- Preserve readable position, contact, pacing, fatigue, clothing or mess state, aftercare, and consequence where relevant.
- Do not turn this framework into a live scene by itself or into instructional real-world sexual content.
