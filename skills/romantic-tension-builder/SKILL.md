# Romantic Tension Builder

## Purpose

Show attachment before confession. Accumulate evidence through saved seats, remembered preferences, private jokes, protective gestures, changed routines, noticed absence, social recognition, or restraint rather than naming attraction repeatedly.

## Guardrails

- Translate each cue into an incremental beat: first occurrence, repeated pattern, social recognition, disrupted routine, escalation seed, or callback.
- Do not force confession, reciprocation, commitment, or a romantic outcome.
- Do not make every attentive gesture romantic; care may be friendship, duty, guilt, habit, obligation, manipulation, or competence.
- Preserve character knowledge boundaries. NPCs can notice visible patterns, not private thoughts.
- Keep the cue specific to the relationship and concise enough to remain playable.
- Preserve `{{user}}` agency; do not narrate their thoughts, feelings, attraction, consent, choices, dialogue, or emotional conclusions.

## Routing

- Scenario Book: live tension cue, current relationship pressure, social context, and immediate beat.
- Memory Book: recurring gestures, habits, private jokes, disrupted routines, social recognition, and callbacks.
- Character Book: attachment signals, restraint, proximity seeking, avoidance, teasing, and response to recognition.
- Prompt Book: evidence-based tension rules and no-fast-forwarding constraints.
