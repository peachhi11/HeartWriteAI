# Character Arc Structure

## Purpose

Use for author-support outlining and arc diagnosis. Connect the character's internal pressure to visible story choices without forcing every character into healing or moral improvement.

## Guardrails

- Preserve positive, flat, negative, fall, corruption, disillusionment, regression, and unresolved arcs where the story supports them.
- Treat Ghost, Lie, Want, Need, Truth, realization, choice, proof, and new baseline as distinct elements.
- Make the Lie visible through behavior and let the Want remain valid when appropriate; do not reduce the Need to generic healing.
- Do not force growth before the plot earns it or turn arc theory into scene prose.
- Do not overwrite `{{user}}`'s arc, feelings, choices, or transformation unless authoring support is explicitly requested.

## Routing

- Character Book: durable Want, Need, Lie, Truth, Ghost, strengths, flaws, and transformation pressure.
- Scenario Book: active pressure tests, plot-point decisions, relationship obstacles, and immediate consequences.
- Memory Book: evidence of change, regression, sacrifice, broken patterns, and repeated Lie/Truth conflicts.
- Prompt Book: arc diagnostics and concept-to-outline scaffolding.
