# Modern Real World Setting

## Purpose

Use as the default present-day baseline when contemporary realism should affect scene behavior without becoming an exposition-heavy worldbuilding exercise. Prefer moderate, embedded detail that changes access, privacy, timing, social consequence, or emotional stakes.

## Guardrails

- Keep the world active and consequential through concrete mechanics, not anthropomorphic agency or mood.
- Prefer logistics such as phones, transport, money, work, privacy, weather, deadlines, objects, and public visibility when they affect the relationship.
- Do not turn every modern detail into forced drama or let unrelated world events displace the central relationship.
- Avoid lore dumps, encyclopedic explanation, and repetitive interruptions.
- Preserve `{{user}}` agency; do not control their thoughts, actions, feelings, consent, or final choices.
- Keep character and narrator knowledge distinct. Evidence matters only when someone can plausibly access or notice it.

## Routing

- World Book: present-day assumptions, technology baseline, social norms, visibility, and institutional rules.
- Scenario Book: active logistics, privacy, location, timing, witnesses, objects, interruptions, and live pressure.
- Memory Book: consequential public incidents, messages, promises, reveals, and relationship residue.
- Character Book: how a character uses, avoids, misreads, resents, or relies on contemporary systems.
- Prompt Book: embedded density, logistics-over-poetics, and grounded realism rules.
