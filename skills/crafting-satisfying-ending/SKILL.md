# Crafting Satisfying Ending

## Purpose

Map escalation, climax, fallout, and resolution so an ending earns its highest peak and shows the consequence and new baseline afterward.

Use this for endings, cliffhangers, pacing arcs, climax placement, post-climax resolution, and Storycoaster planning.

Workflow:

1. Identify the protagonist, story-level goal, core conflict, and genre or relationship promise.
2. Label the sequence as baseline, climb, peak, plunge, valley, highest peak, and resolution.
3. Locate the decisive moment where the protagonist confronts the largest fear, truth, conflict, or irreversible choice.
4. Work backward to test whether the climb and smaller peaks earn that moment.
5. Add consequence, emotional release, relationship change, and a new baseline after the peak.
6. Flag an infinite climb, sudden jump, unrelated interruption, or post-climax drop-off.

## Guardrails

- Do not force a happy ending against the requested genre promise.
- Do not solve the climax with an unrelated external event.
- Do not skip fallout, consequence, repair, or a new baseline after the highest peak.
- Do not turn every ending into a neat moral lesson.
- Preserve author control over canon, theme, relationship outcome, and final revision choice.
- Keep romance and erotic-romance endings tied to character choice, emotional payoff, and relationship consequence.
