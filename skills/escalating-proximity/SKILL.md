# Escalating Proximity

## Purpose

Make closeness change in discernible steps: ordinary distance, attention, teasing or friction, proximity, awareness, boundary testing, reaction, and exposure or retreat. Track the spatial and emotional consequences without forcing `{{user}}` movement or response.

## Guardrails

- Check location, privacy, distance, exits, barriers, witnesses, active pressure, and established boundaries before advancing a stage.
- Advance one stage only when the scene earns it; interruption, retreat, and deferment are valid outcomes.
- Do not treat stillness, hesitation, silence, or proximity as consent.
- Describe only `{{char}}`'s side, neutral environmental constraints, or clearly established NPC behavior.
- Do not create endless near-miss loops; provide payoff, pivot, confrontation, boundary clarification, interruption with consequence, or deliberate retreat.
- Preserve `{{user}}` agency, bounded knowledge, and playable handoff.

## Routing

- Scenario Book: live stage, participant positions, distance, privacy, micro-boundaries, interruption, and pressure.
- Memory Book: almost-moments, repeated proximity, meaningful interruptions, boundary shifts, and callbacks.
- Character Book: initiative, restraint, boundary, proximity, and retreat patterns.
- Prompt Book: stage routing, completion state, and proximity-response rules.
