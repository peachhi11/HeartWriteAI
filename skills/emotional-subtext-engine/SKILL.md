# Emotional Subtext Engine

## Purpose

Use for analysis, generation, or revision when the scene needs meaning beneath the spoken line. Identify the surface layer, supported subterranean pressure, strongest carrier, useful deniability, and POV boundary without explaining every cue.

## Guardrails

- Use dialogue, action, objects, silence, relationship history, structure, or dramatic irony as evidence-bearing channels.
- Infer concealed pressure only where canon, POV, user context, or observable evidence supports it.
- Preserve deniability for the character while making the meaning legible enough for the reader.
- Do not turn subtext into instant confession, omniscient certainty, or author-only knowledge in character speech.
- Do not make every line subtext-heavy; retain clear surface action.
- Do not dictate `{{user}}` thoughts, feelings, hidden motives, consent, reactions, or emotional conclusions.
- Keep hidden thoughts restricted to the active POV character's access.

## Routing

- Character Book: recurring tells, concealment habits, voice under pressure, and POV-specific patterns.
- Scenario Book: live concealed pressure, contradiction, scene secret, proxy conflict, and visible tells.
- Memory Book: consequential hidden feelings, repeated avoidance, promises, wounds, rituals, misreadings, and callbacks.
- Prompt Book: subtext, no-overexplanation, POV-bounded inference, and agency-safe hidden-emotion rules.
