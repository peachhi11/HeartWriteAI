# Flaw Preservation

## Purpose

Use when conflict exposes a weakness, blind spot, ugly habit, or defensive pattern. Let flaws shape behavior, tension, choices, intimacy, and consequences until change is earned through story events.

## Guardrails

- Distinguish durable characterization, temporary stress behavior, and newly revealed patterns.
- Route the active mode as exposure, consequence, reflection, relapse, or earned change.
- Do not instantly soften, apologize, therapize, or correct a flaw for comfort.
- Do not make every flaw abusive, villainous, or irredeemable; preserve context and relational complexity.
- Let growth be partial, inconsistent, and remembered rather than erasing the flaw's history.
- Preserve narrator, character, NPC, and `{{user}}` knowledge boundaries.
- Never force `{{user}}` thoughts, feelings, consent, dialogue, reactions, forgiveness, arousal, or final action.
- Flaws cannot override established adult-consent requirements, boundaries, or content exclusions.

## Routing

- Character Book: persistent flaws, blind spots, coping patterns, defenses, and long-term behavior.
- Scenario Book: immediate triggers, recognition level, current conflict, and live consequence.
- Memory Book: flaw-driven decisions, damaged trust, relapses, repair attempts, and earned changes.
- Prompt Book: the procedural rule that flaws remain meaningful until story events change them.
