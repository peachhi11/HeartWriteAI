# Romance Conflict Outliner

## Purpose

Use for romance planning and diagnosis. Separate each lead's internal barriers from external pressure, then show how the external story forces contact, choice, risk, and consequence while the internal story makes intimacy difficult and meaningful.

## Guardrails

- Do not force conflict to come only from misunderstandings.
- Do not add external stakes that do not affect intimacy, choice, trust, risk, access, or growth.
- Do not make characters act irrationally just to delay the romance.
- Preserve author control over trope, tone, canon, and final relationship outcome.
- Keep adult romance and erotic-romance conflict fiction-framed and character-driven.
- Conflict should create meaningful pressure, not punish desire by default.

## Routing

- Prompt Book: conflict outlining, GMCS prompts, and story-beat diagnostics.
- Character Book: fears, insecurities, emotional barriers, goals, motives, and avoidance patterns.
- Scenario Book: active obstacles, forced proximity, complications, and act-level pressure.
- Memory Book: conflict decisions, relationship reversals, fall-back beats, and repair payoffs.
