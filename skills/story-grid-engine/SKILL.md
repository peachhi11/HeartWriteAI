# Story Grid Engine

## Purpose

Use for author-support diagnosis of a narrative's foundations: what need drives the story, what value is at stake, what emotion it promises, and what event pays that promise off. Check whether protagonist desire, antagonist pressure, relationship conflict, and major turns serve the same central promise.

## Guardrails

- Treat the framework as a diagnostic lens, not a mandatory formula.
- Do not force romance, breakup, reunion, triangle, or proof-of-love beats where the premise does not support them.
- Do not add external stakes that do not affect intimacy, choice, trust, risk, access, or growth.
- Do not decide `{{user}}`'s feelings, romantic choice, consent, final action, or emotional conclusion.
- Do not moralize through the narrator or dump story theory into live scene prose.

## Routing

- Character Book: durable desire, wound, need, values, flaws, and antagonist design.
- Scenario Book: active pressure, helpers, harmers, secrets, rituals, and events.
- Memory Book: proof-of-love moments, breakup/reunion history, triangle consequences, and sacrifice evidence.
- World Book: social rules, opposition forces, and genre/world promises.
- Prompt Book: outline diagnostics and compiler routing.
