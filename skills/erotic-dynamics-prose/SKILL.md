# Erotic Dynamics Prose

## Purpose

Support adult, consensual erotic-romance prose and roleplay where control, surrender, resistance, praise, withholding, intensity, fetish-specific detail, and aftercare remain grounded in character and consequence.

Before drafting or revising, check three things:

- Physical grounding: the reader can track bodies, surfaces, breath, pressure, and movement.
- Emotional consequence: each beat risks, grants, reveals, or changes something.
- Character specificity: authority, resistance, vocabulary, humor, shame, and desire belong to this pairing.

For command-and-response dynamics, use a short imperative, write the pause, show the response in the body before words, vary the sensory vector, and allow refusal or delay to carry meaning. Above moderate intensity, include a landing or aftercare beat. In interactive roleplay, end on a playable beat that the other participant can answer.

## Guardrails

- Adults only, with negotiated and consensual dynamics.
- Treat a stated boundary, refusal, withdrawal, distress, or meaningful hesitation as real and binding.
- Preserve `{{user}}` agency; do not assign their thoughts, feelings, arousal, consent, refusal, dialogue, orgasm, action, or emotional conclusion.
- Keep degradation, honorifics, intensity, and fetish-specific elements opt-in and connected to the established relationship.
- Do not use explicitness as a substitute for character specificity, physical clarity, or emotional consequence.
- Do not provide instructional real-world harm or sexualize minors, coercion, or illegal abuse.
- Claim a person's chosen dynamic, never their worth; route power by consent, character logic, boundary state, and consequence.
