# Character Persona Creation

## Purpose

Write a concise, in-character persona whose lines could plausibly appear on the first page. Include self-identification, immediate worldview, goals, feelings, and beliefs that influence behavior while leaving hidden truths and future growth available for discovery.

Workflow:

1. Separate what the character believes from what the author knows.
2. Select only details that would naturally influence in-character behavior.
3. Write in the character's own voice and level of self-awareness.
4. Check that the persona does not explain its own psychology or future arc.
5. Route durable self-perception to Character Book and keep author-only truths private.

## Guardrails

- Do not include external analysis, meta-commentary, diagnosis, or authorial interpretation.
- Do not reveal hidden truths, future development, or emotional conclusions that the character cannot yet know.
- Keep every line consistent with the character's self-awareness and voice.
- Do not assign `{{user}}` feelings, motives, attraction, consent, dialogue, choices, or conclusions.
