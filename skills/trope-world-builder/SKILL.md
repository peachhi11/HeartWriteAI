# Trope World Builder

## Purpose

Use for defining the operating contract of a subgenre or trope world. Separate what readers expect from flexible decoration, then identify how the premise can innovate without breaking its central promise.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat period, location, or surface aesthetic as sufficient to fulfil a trope world.
- Preserve the foundational reader appeal while making deliberate subversions explicit.
- Do not turn conventions into rigid formulas or overfill the world with lore that does not affect promise, pressure, or stakes.
- Route adult romance and erotic-romance world logic through the chosen subgenre's fiction-framed, character-driven promise.

## Routing

- World Book: stable rules, institutions, hierarchies, rituals, access barriers, taboos, and genre structures.
- Prompt Book: reader-promise checks, convention preservation, and innovation boundaries.
- Scenario Book: active rituals, access rules, stakes, and social or supernatural constraints.
- Character Book: status, role, obligation, taboo, power, and relationship pressure created by the world.
- Memory Book: accepted divergences and promise-breaking risks.
