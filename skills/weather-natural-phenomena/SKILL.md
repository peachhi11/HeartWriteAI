# Weather Natural Phenomena

## Purpose

Use for worldbuilding rules or live scene pressure involving climate, seasons, natural hazards, unusual phenomena, or magical environmental systems. Define manifestation, scope, recurrence, intensity, limits, and practical consequences before escalating the phenomenon.

## Guardrails

- Make realistic, speculative, supernatural, and magical systems internally consistent with rules, limits, recurrence, and consequences.
- Do not introduce sudden disasters or magical weather solely to force drama.
- Do not contradict established World Book or Scenario Book facts.
- Foreground environmental conditions only when they affect action, perception, culture, danger, intimacy, or consequence.
- Do not anthropomorphize weather, silence, rooms, cities, or landscapes.
- Do not use the environment to decide `{{user}}` thoughts, feelings, bodily responses, consent, dialogue, or final actions.
- Do not let a phenomenon solve plot problems without an established mechanism, cost, limit, or consequence.

## Routing

- World Book: climate, seasons, natural laws, magical systems, hazards, and cultural adaptations.
- Scenario Book: current condition, visibility, terrain, travel impact, risk, and active environmental pressure.
- Memory Book: significant disasters, rare phenomena, location scars, and recurring seasonal associations.
- Prompt Book: rules for when environmental systems enter generation or stay backgrounded.
