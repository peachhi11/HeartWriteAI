## Dirty Talk Registers

These registers are useful for prompt compilation because they describe the function of the line, not just its explicitness.

### Narration And Observation

Function:

- names what is happening
- makes attention visible
- turns observation into direction
- helps shy or inexperienced speakers avoid over-scripted dialogue

Compiler use:

- attach dialogue to the current physical action
- make the line specific to body position, response, pressure, or emotional shift
- avoid generic reusable filler

### Command And Control

Function:

- gives a short action instruction
- changes posture, pace, gaze, position, or permission
- lets silence after the command carry authority

Compiler use:

- map command intensity to relationship trust and heat level
- keep commands short when the scene is physically busy
- pair commands with visible compliance, resistance, bratting, hesitation, or correction

### Praise And Reward

Function:

- rewards an action, effort, surrender, patience, bravery, service, or honesty
- stabilizes harder registers
- makes power exchange feel desired rather than mechanical

Compiler use:

- praise the act, not only the body
- vary praise by character voice: stern, warm, teasing, reverent, blunt, possessive
- use praise as a landing after correction, humiliation, denial, or intense play

### Possession And Claiming

Function:

- creates exclusivity, belonging, protection, pride, or objectification depending on frame
- can be affirming, dark-romantic, jealous, or controlling depending on StoryBook context

Compiler use:

- route with `claimFrame`: devotion, protection, jealousy, ownership play, public risk, insecurity pressure
- do not assume the same language is positive or negative without reception/context
- connect claiming language to action: touch, gaze, public behavior, private title, rule, or aftermath

### Teasing, Denial, And Verbal Edging

Function:

- delays gratification
- uses anticipation as pressure
- invites asking, begging, waiting, or playful frustration

Compiler use:

- track the agreed window: light tease, short denial, long denial, orgasm control, chastity, no-denial
- distinguish frustration-as-play from genuine frustration
- let release, softening, or reward land as part of the arc

### Begging, Permission, And Request

Function:

- turns want into spoken participation
- lets the receiver name desire
- gives the leader a cue to grant, delay, redirect, or tease

Compiler use:

- map to user agency carefully: never invent `{{user}}` begging unless impersonation assist is active
- for `{{char}}`, begging can reveal shame, need, obedience, bravado, desperation, or earned vulnerability
- use one-line requests during high-intensity scenes; save longer confession for slower beats

### Correction, Discipline, And Brat-Taming

Function:

- responds to playful defiance, rule-breaking, testing, or mischief
- restores structure
- turns pushback into a game when mutually welcomed

Compiler use:

- route by reception: playful, aroused, testing, hurt, resistant, boundary
- keep real anger separate from play-anger
- close with praise, forgiveness, reset, consequence, or negotiated next step

### Degradation, Humiliation, And Objectification

Function:

- creates heat through status drop, shame-play, use-object framing, exposure, or taboo language
- can be affectionate, erotic, brutal, comic, or wounding depending on agreement and character

Compiler use:

- requires explicit dynamic fit in fiction settings and adult-only context
- route names and insults word-by-word; do not infer tolerance from general heat level
- land with aftercare, praise, ordinary naming, touch, or scene reset when intensity is high
- keep identity-targeted or platform-sensitive language backend/context-only unless explicitly allowed by platform and story frame

### Worship, Service, And Gentle Authority

Function:

- makes dominance warm, devotional, attentive, and structured
- lets service and surrender feel like care rather than dungeon protocol

Compiler use:

- useful for gentle femdom, caregiver dynamics, service submission, romantic worship, praise kink
- avoid turning every soft scene into formal BDSM staging
- route worship through body language, domestic care, kneeling, touch, verbal devotion, or practical service depending on the story

### Text, Voice Note, And Distance Register

Function:

- turns distance into anticipation
- allows slower calibration and rehearsal
- supports all-day arcs and asynchronous scenes

Compiler use:

- Scenario Book should track message thread state, delay, missed replies, privacy, screenshots/photos boundaries
- keep silence ambiguous until context resolves it: anticipation, anxiety, busy life, avoidance, boundary, or loss of interest
- voice notes can carry tone and command without overexplaining

## Vocabulary Morphology Banks

### Name And Address Register Spectrum

Useful register families:

- everyday endearment: soft, romantic, low-protocol, often usable outside scenes
- playful nickname: silly, private, teasing, often relationship-specific
- food/consumption nickname: warm objectification, affectionate appetite, soft possession
- praise name: approval attached to behavior or identity
- service title: status through usefulness, competence, task, ritual, or response
- pet-play name: affection, training, caretaking, animal-coded play, praise response

## Source-Mining Takeaways

- Dirty talk is best modeled as register plus function, not as phrase lists.
- Dominance and submission are not gendered defaults; gender changes vocabulary, not the underlying route.
- Praise, command, teasing, denial, degradation, worship, service, and correction can all be positive, conflicted, or wounding depending on context.
- Aftercare/drop material is a core memory-system feature, not an optional BDSM appendix.
- Relationship structures need their own agreement/knowledge map; open/poly/swinging/hotwife/cuckold stories break if the app treats them as simple two-person dynamics.
- Kink should not automatically turn a scene into formal high-protocol BDSM. Many dynamics occur as native relationship behavior, flirtation, comfort, conflict, care, or erotic play without dungeon staging.
- User-facing language should stay readable and fiction-friendly; backend routing can carry the heavier semantics.
