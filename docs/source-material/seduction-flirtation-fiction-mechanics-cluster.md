Use these files for narrative language, scene moves, relationship pressure, damaged-character logic, flirtation registers, and routing tests. ## Fiction Routing Principle

The vocabulary bank supplies possible moves. The StoryBook decides what they mean.

The same line or behavior can route differently depending on:

- character intent
- relationship history
- trust level
- genre and trope
- scene privacy
- target sensitivity
- current emotional pressure
- `{{user}}` reception
- aftermath and repair pattern

Use this route shape:

```ts
fictionMove =
  sourceBehavior
  + characterVoice
  + relationshipDynamic
  + scenePressure
  + targetSensitivity
  + userReception
  + aftermathNeed
```

## User Move Trigger Additions

Add or extend trigger families:

- `approach`: closes distance, steps in, leans closer, initiates contact
- `verbal_invitation`: invites more, asks to continue, asks to be shown
- `tease_response`: laughs, smirks, teases back, escalates the joke
- `brat_cue`: challenges, dares, resists playfully, eye-rolls, taunts
- `deflection`: jokes away emotion, changes subject, hides vulnerability behind sass
- `hesitation`: pauses, falters, looks away, freezes at threshold
- `withdrawal`: steps back, pulls away, creates distance
- `boundary_signal`: says no, stop, pause, not that, do not joke about that
- `repair_invitation`: softens, explains why it hurt, asks for reassurance
- `aftercare_cue`: reaches for grounding, comfort, water, quiet, reassurance

## Prompt Compiler Implications

This cluster should feed three separate compiler layers:

1. **Semantic route layer**
   Tags the move as flirtation, fixation, challenge, jealousy, curiosity, presentation, vocal register, or repair.

2. **Fiction register layer**
   Converts backend taxonomy into genre-appropriate prose language: playful, dark, tender, sharp, obsessive, vulnerable, comic, ashamed, hungry, or reverent.

3. **Context safety and agency layer**
   Keeps `{{user}}` agency intact unless impersonation assist is explicitly active. The app may suggest possible user moves, but the prompt should not assume hidden feelings, consent, dialogue, arousal, or final choice.

## Richer Register Seed Banks

These seeds give the compiler prose-ready registers for flirtation, seduction, fixation, texting, body language, voice, metaphor, and status play. They should be routed through StoryBook context before use.

### Playful Teasing And Sharp Banter

Core verbs:

- teases, needles, provokes, deflects, tests, dares, baits
- smirks, tilts, circles, lingers, withholds, relents
- flicks a look, lets silence stretch, makes them work for the answer

Tone seeds:

- affectionate sting
- dry little cut
- sugar over a blade
- mischief with a pulse under it
- challenge wearing a smile
- insult that asks to be answered
- provocation that leaves room to play back

Line-shape seeds:

- says the opposite of the tender thing
- praises by pretending not to praise
- makes the compliment look like an accusation
- turns attraction into an argument
- tests whether they will chase, laugh, retreat, or bite back
- gives them an opening and pretends it was an accident

Reception routes:

- welcomed: laughter, eye contact, counter-tease, moving closer
- bratting: challenge, defiance, playful refusal, exaggerated offense
- conflicted: pause, flush, uncertain smile, self-protective joke
- hurt: silence, withdrawal, brittle politeness, topic change
- repair-needed: softening, explanation, reassurance, changed tone

### Curiosity Hooks And Texting Pressure

Hook verbs:

- hints, withholds, delays, circles, drops, reveals, revises
- sends, deletes, rewrites, waits, leaves unread, answers too late
- opens a loop, changes the subject, returns to it hours later

Text-message seed shapes:

- one unfinished thought that begs a reply
- a joke with a serious underside
- a casual message sent at an emotionally suspicious hour
- a question too specific to be innocent
- a confession disguised as logistics
- a dare with plausible deniability
- a callback only the two of them understand

Pressure routes:

- curiosity as flirtation
- curiosity as surveillance
- curiosity as vulnerability
- curiosity as bait
- curiosity as secret-sharing
- curiosity as delayed confession

### Obsession, Infatuation, And Fixation Register

Fixation verbs:

- tracks, memorizes, circles, revisits, replays, catalogues, notices
- hoards details, reads patterns, checks for signs, measures distance
- returns to the thought despite himself

Desire-pressure seeds:

- remembers too much
- notices what no one else bothers to see
- treats coincidence like evidence
- makes restraint look like indifference
- wants proof, then mistrusts it when it arrives
- mistakes longing for strategy
- makes devotion sound like threat because softness would expose him

Dark-romance route distinctions:

- possessive devotion: wants closeness, protection, witness, exclusivity
- obsessive control: wants certainty, access, obedience, proof
- mutually obsessive: both characters intensify, mirror, and challenge the fixation
- jealousy spiral: fear of replacement becomes accusation or pursuit
- redemption pressure: fixation forces the character to choose care over control

### Body Language And Spatial Dynamics

Approach verbs:

- steps in, angles closer, narrows the gap, leans down, crowds gently
- braces a hand nearby, blocks without trapping, offers an exit with his body
- turns fully toward them, lowers his voice, waits inside their space

Withdrawal verbs:

- steps back, slips free, angles away, checks the door, folds inward
- tucks hands away, looks past them, makes the room larger
- creates distance with furniture, task, humor, or silence

Tension cues:

- eyes hold too long
- mouth almost moves before words arrive
- hand pauses before contact
- breath changes before posture does
- laughter comes out quieter than expected
- a practical gesture turns intimate by lingering one second too long

Spatial route seeds:

- proximity as invitation
- proximity as dominance
- distance as self-protection
- distance as punishment
- stillness as an invitation, a pause, or a need to read context before escalating
- stillness as fear
- movement as escape
- movement as pursuit

### Voice, Tonality, And Register Shifts

Voice texture seeds:

- low, rough, careful, lazy, clipped, amused, solemn
- warm at the edges, cold in the middle
- too even to be calm
- soft enough to be dangerous
- casual in a way that means rehearsed
- intimate because no one else can hear it

Delivery behaviors:

- pauses before the important word
- drops the joke when it stops working
- says the tender thing like an accusation
- uses a pet name only when control slips
- stops using the nickname when the scene turns serious
- lets silence ask the question

Prompt routes:

- soft voice as reassurance
- low voice as command
- clipped voice as restraint
- amused voice as deflection
- formal voice as emotional distance
- rough voice as desire breaking containment

### Metaphor, Absurdity, And Character Humor

Metaphor functions:

- flirtation through absurd comparison
- insult softened by impossibility
- vulnerability displaced into image
- desire hidden inside joke logic
- class/status signal through reference style
- character voice marker

Seed shapes:

- compares the person to something inconveniently unforgettable
- uses a ridiculous image to avoid saying beautiful
- makes a metaphor too specific to be generic
- turns an ordinary object into private symbolism
- gives the relationship a running joke that slowly becomes sincere
- says the grand feeling through a small, odd detail

Register tags:

- absurdist flirt
- poetic indirectness
- deadpan romantic
- profane tender
- academic deflection
- melodramatic-on-purpose
- hyper-specific sensory metaphor

### Status, Challenge, And Polarity Register

Backend concepts:

- status display
- challenge
- qualification
- push-pull
- frame control
- approval seeking
- social proof
- pursuit/retreat rhythm
- power reversal

Fiction translations:

- refuses to beg, but keeps showing up
- makes them prove they mean it
- tests the promise without admitting it matters
- lets the silence decide who moves first
- gives ground in action, not in words
- offers obedience with a look that still argues
- turns the chase into a negotiation neither names

Character use:

- cocky flirt
- guarded romantic lead
- socially strategic character
- status-obsessed rival
- damaged man testing whether love survives pressure
- user persona who answers dominance with wit rather than compliance
